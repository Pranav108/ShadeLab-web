Privacy policy

Room for color. Respect for your information.

Effective and last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. No account is needed. If you contact us, we use your message to help you. Website hosting involves the technical information needed to serve and protect these pages.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how ShadeLab handles information in the game and on this website. The operator is the ShadeLab developer. For privacy questions or requests, email pranvcoder@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

ShadeLab stores your puzzle state, completed levels, undo history, tutorial progress, navigation state and preferences on your device. Preferences include sound, music, haptics, reduced motion and color symbols. These files let you continue where you left off. You do not need to create an account, and we do not run a server that receives or synchronizes your game progress.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

The current game does not integrate an advertising service, advertising identifier tracking, in-app purchases or developer-configured behavioral analytics. Unity cloud analytics, advertising and cloud diagnostics services are disabled in the current project. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Apple and device services

ShadeLab is built with Unity and runs on Apple devices. Apple may separately process App Store transactions, installation information or device diagnostics according to your device settings and its own privacy policy. Device backups may include locally stored game files, depending on your Apple backup settings. ShadeLab does not operate its own cloud-save or account service. Read Apple’s privacy information.

When you contact us

If you email pranvcoder@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and any details you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Email is handled through our email provider, Google, under its privacy policy.

This website

This website does not add advertising trackers, audience analytics, contact forms or visitor accounts. Fonts and game artwork are served with the website rather than loaded from third-party font services. GitHub Pages and its infrastructure providers may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser information, request time and URL, to deliver and protect the site. Essential platform or security cookies may be used by the hosting provider. We do not add marketing cookies or use website requests to track your gameplay. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for hosting-provider information.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information you send by email is handled by the email service used to deliver and respond to your message. Technical website requests are handled by hosting and security providers. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address misuse or security incidents.

Retention and deletion

Game progress and preferences stay on your device until the app’s stored data is removed. On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain it. Copies in device backups are controlled through your Apple backup settings and may remain after app deletion. We cannot retrieve or delete local game files remotely. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal or security needs. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence. Hosting providers apply their own operational and security retention practices.

Your choices and requests

You can play without registering or sending us personal information. You can change game preferences in Settings, manage device diagnostics and backups through Apple settings, and remove local game data by deleting the app. To ask about information you sent us, request access or correction, or request deletion of support correspondence, contact pranvcoder@gmail.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify that a request concerns your own information.

Children’s privacy

The game does not require players, including children, to submit personal information or create an account. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, please contact us so we can address the request and delete it as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game, website or our data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Changes that require permission will be handled before the relevant collection or use begins.

Contact

For ShadeLab support and privacy matters: pranvcoder@gmail.com.
