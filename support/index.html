Common questions

How do I pour?

Tap a source bottle, then an empty bottle or one with a matching top color. The game pours consecutive matching top layers into the available space.

Why is a finished bottle waiting?

A sealed bottle packs when its color matches an active order. Check the next-order preview and continue sorting until that color reaches an active slot.

What does Undo restore?

Undo reverses the previous pour and its connected sealing, packing and unlock changes together.

How do I reveal a covered bottle?

Complete the number of new bottles shown on its cover. The cover’s counter decreases as you make those completions.

Can I come back to my puzzle later?

Yes. Your current puzzle and preferences are saved locally. Use Save and exit from the pause menu when you want to take a break.

Can I play without relying only on color?

Color symbols are available in Settings. You can also choose reduced motion, and adjust sound, music and haptics.

Does ShadeLab sync between devices?

ShadeLab does not have an account or its own cloud-sync service. Progress is stored on the device where you play. Apple device backups may behave according to your backup settings.

How do I report a problem?

Email pranvcoder@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, level number and a short description. A screenshot can help. Please avoid including sensitive information.
