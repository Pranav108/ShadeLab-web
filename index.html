A simple start.
Room to think.

  1. Choose your bottle

    Tap a source, then an empty bottle or one with the same color on top. Matching top layers pour together, up to the space available.

  2. Bring a color together

    Four matching layers seal a bottle. It packs when a matching order is active; otherwise it waits safely sealed.

  3. Make your next move

    Keep an eye on the next order. Use Undo when you want to rethink a pour, or pause and return later.

Make yourself comfortable

Choose sound, music and haptics. Turn on reduced motion or use color symbols to help distinguish the layers.

Your progress stays with you

Your puzzle and settings are saved on your device. No account is needed to play. Read our privacy policy.
